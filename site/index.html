Google Device Price Tracker

Pixel, Pixel Watch, Pixel Buds, Fitbit & Nest/Home prices across 5 markets — updated daily. Last refresh: loading…

Google Store prices are scraped directly from each market's store.google.com storefront. Competitor prices marked with a value are scraped live from that platform; entries marked "Check price →" couldn't be scraped automatically (most retail sites block or JS-render their pricing) and link straight to a search for that product instead of a guessed number. Discount/markup badges compare a competitor's scraped price against the Google Store "from" price for the same product family, not necessarily the identical colour/storage SKU. See the project README for scraper details and limitations.
